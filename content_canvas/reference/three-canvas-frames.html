An applet

The same concept, driven by the learner through five stages — reveal, a slider, a choice group carrying a trap, judged feedback, and a snapshot that consolidates. Action blue now appears, and only on things you can actually touch.

Applet · thirds

An interstitial, from the actual kit

Not a drawing of one. This mounts Interstitials.create() from the published interstitials.js — the same bytes a seat fetches and the generator copies into every bundle. It is the component the whole conformance pass was about, so the page should be running it rather than imitating it.

Interstitial · trap
It consumes the token bridgeThe kit's own tokens are now defined from §26 — --itl-cyan: var(--accent, …) — so it follows the room's palette and needs no consent request when the palette moves.canvas §26
It paints no fieldTransparent at every root, so it sits on whatever the room paints. Toggle the field above and watch it hold.canvas §0.1 · contract §7.10
Its radii and weights are on the ladderTracks and switches at 999, keys at 20, panels at 16 — and a content weight of 500 that it never used to set at all.canvas §4 · §2
What this page is not. It links Roboto from a font host so it renders anywhere. A real applet does the opposite — it declares no font at all and inherits the pinned face from the room, per §25 check 3 and §26. Bundling a font per applet would be the error.
Boards are light in both page themes on purpose: canvas has no dark mode (R21), so the documentation shell follows your theme and the boards do not.
One tension these frames surface, measured rather than argued. §0.2 says the room scales the whole board; R18 sets a 44px hit-target floor. Below a scale of about 1.0 they disagree — at a 600px viewport these boards render at 0.58 and a 44px control measures 26px. Nothing in the CSS is wrong and no check catches it. Canvas states no minimum scale, so today it is the room's job not to hand a board less space than it was authored for. Worth a rule.
